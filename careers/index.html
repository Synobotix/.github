HOW WE WORK

Four engineers, one rule

Close to the client, not a bench

You work directly with the client's team on one program at a time. You are not a pool to be reassigned when something else needs coverage.

Embedded in their team

Their repo, their stand-ups, their review board. We work inside the client's process rather than around it.

Built to leave

Every engagement ends with their engineers able to retrain and redeploy without us. Teaching is part of the deliverable, not a favour.

Write it down or it did not happen

A safety reviewer has to be able to read the envelope spec and agree with it. If it is not written and tested, it is not shipped.

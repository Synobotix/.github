## PHASE 2 : Commenting strategy

### 2.1 Comment types

```
COMMENT TYPES:

[ ] Value-add (best)
   "J'aime beaucoup. Ajouter..."

[ ] Question
   "Interesting. Qu'en penses-tu de..."

[ ] Story
   "Similar experience..."

[ ] Agreement
   "Totally daccord avec..."

[ ] Question for OP
   "Great post. Question..."

Avoid:
- "Nice!"
- "Great!"
- Just emoji
- Aggressive disagree
```

### 2.2 Comment formulas

```
FORMULA VALUE:

Paragraph 1: Agreement/Appreciation
Paragraph 2: Add value (story/example)
Paragraph 3: Question for author

Length: 2-4 lignes
Timing: Post within 1-2h (catch early)
```

## PHASE 5 : Daily routine

### 5.1 Routine

```
ROUTINE:

Morning (15 min):
- Engage key accounts
- 5-10 meaningful comments
- Accept connections

Midday (10 min):
- Response to comments on YOUR posts

Evening (5 min):
- Schedule next day engagement

Engagement loop:
- Your posts → Their comments → Reply to them
- Their posts → Your comments → They notice you
```

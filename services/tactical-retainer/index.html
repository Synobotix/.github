ASK A COMPLIANCE QUESTION

QUESTIONS WE GET

Can we start with one month?

The minimum is six months, because the first months buy design rather than a running system. If you want a single month of work with a hard deliverable, that is a Milestone Sprint.

Who owns the model?

You do, including weights, training code and the safety layer. We keep no runtime licence and charge nothing per unit shipped.

Do you work on our hardware?

Yes, and we would rather have two units of it on our bench from week one. Where that is impossible we build a representative HIL rig and say clearly what it does not cover.

What if we have no ML engineers?

That is the normal case. Standing up that team is part of the engagement: profiles, screening, interviews and onboarding onto the system we built together.

How is progress reported?

Weekly written note, monthly demo on hardware, and a running artefact repository your program lead can read without us in the room.

Will you tell us to stop?

If the requirement cannot be met inside your envelope, yes, in writing, with what would have to change. We would rather lose the month than the reference.

Bring us the requirement, not the solution

Send what the machine has to do, the target platform and the date you are working to. Within two working days you get a straight answer: retainer or sprint, the shape of the first quarter, and what it would cost.

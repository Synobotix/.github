/ WHERE THIS CAME FROM

Two years of watching good models fail on the floor.

I started Synobotix because of a pattern I kept meeting from the inside: a perception stack that demoed beautifully, then drifted to 400ms under load, then could not be certified because nobody could put a bound on it.

I built the firm around that gap. Not the research, not the product. The engineering in between: quantization that survives validation, kernels fused against a real thermal envelope, and control paths that degrade to a known state rather than to whatever the network last emitted.

/ HOW WE ENGINEER

01

Latency is a safety property

A loop you cannot bound is a hazard you cannot document. We treat p99 as a requirement with a number next to it, agreed before work starts.

02

Determinism over cleverness

The supervisor is deliberately dumb: a finite state machine, fully testable, independent of the policy it guards. Boring is the feature.

03

Measure on the target

Workstation numbers are marketing. Every figure we publish comes off the deployed board, thermally soaked, at the duty cycle you actually run.

04

Hand back the toolchain

Engagements end with your team able to re-run the benchmarks and rebuild every artifact without us in the room.
